## 1. Lattā

### 1.1 The verses

**(a) Phaladīpikā 26.42–47** (Phal-H lines 17472–17515; C). As the file reads:

> रवेद्वदिशनक्षत्रं भूसुतस्य तृतीयकम् । गुरोः षट्तारकं चैव शनेरष्टमतारकम् ॥४२॥ एतेषां च पुरोलत्ता पृष्ठलत्ताः प्रकीर्त्तिताः । शुक्रस्य पञ्चमं तारं चन्द्रजस्य तु सप्तमम् ॥४३॥ राहोस्तु नवमं चैव द्वाविंशं भं हिमद्युतेः । ग्रहस्थितर्क्षाद्गणयेल्लत्तायां जन्मभे व्यथा ॥४४॥ रवेः सर्वार्थहानिः स्यात्तमसोर्दुःखमुच्यते । मरणं जीवलत्तायां बन्धुनाशो भयावहः ॥४५॥ शुक्रस्य कलहो भ्रंश अनर्थः शशिजस्य तु । चन्द्रस्य तु महाहानिर्लत्तामात्रफलं भवेत् ॥४६॥ सर्वत्र लत्तासाङ्कर्ये द्विगुणत्रिगुणादिकम् । वदेद्दोषफलं नृणां ग्रहाल्लत्ताधिकक्रमात् ॥४७॥

(First word as OCR prints it; I read *raver dvādaśa-nakṣatraṃ*.) The verse gives the numbers and "counted from the nakṣatra the graha stands in" but not the direction: "eteṣāṃ ca puro-lattā pṛṣṭha-lattāḥ prakīrtitāḥ". The direction is the Hindi editor's (Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn forward; Venus, Mercury, Rāhu, Moon backward), and agrees with the MC verse below. Result when it falls on the **janma-bhe**: *vyathā* (pain, disease, weariness, the Hindi). Results by graha: Sun loss of all wealth; Rāhu and Ketu (*tamasoḥ*, dual) sorrow; Jupiter death, loss of kin, fear; Venus quarrel and ruin; Mercury calamity; Moon great loss; Mars and Saturn nothing separate. Several lattās on one nakṣatra multiply the fault (26.47). The editor's examples: Sun in Kṛttikā kicks Citrā (12th), Venus in Jyeṣṭhā kicks Citrā (5th back), Mars in Uttara Phalgunī kicks Citrā (3rd): inclusive count, and none of the examples crosses Abhijit.

**(b) Muhūrtacintāmaṇi, Vivāha prakaraṇa (6), v. 57 in the Pīyūṣadhārā edition (v. 59 in Mahīdhara's Hindi edition)** (MC pdf page 274, MC-H page 107; **P**):

> ज्ञराहुपूर्णेन्दुसिताः स्वपृष्ठे भं सप्तगोजातिशरैर्मितं हि । संलत्तयन्तेऽर्कशनीज्यभौमाः सूर्याष्टतर्काग्निमितं पुरस्तात् ॥५७॥
> *jña-rāhu-pūrṇendu-sitāḥ sva-pṛṣṭhe bhaṃ sapta-go-jāti-śarair mitaṃ hi / saṃlattayante 'rka-śanī-jya-bhaumāḥ sūryāṣṭa-tarkāgni-mitaṃ purastāt.*

Mercury, Rāhu, the **full** Moon and Venus kick the nakṣatra 7, 9 (*go*), 22 (*jāti*), 5 (*śara*) **behind** their own; the Sun, Saturn, Jupiter, Mars the 12, 8, 6 (*tarka*), 3 (*agni*) **ahead**. Pīyūṣadhārā: "एतैर्मितं भं स्वाक्रान्तनक्षत्राद् ज्ञराहुपूर्णेन्दुसिताः स्वपृष्ठे संलत्तयन्ते । यथा—बुधः सप्तमम्, राहुर्नवमम्, पूर्णेन्दुः … द्वाविंशम्" (the count starts at the nakṣatra the graha occupies, *svākrānta-nakṣatrāt*, which is 1); on the Moon: "pūrṇendu = pūrṇimānta-candro dvāviṃśam; tac ca gata-kṛṣṇa-pakṣe pañcamī-ṣaṣṭhī-saptamīnām anyatamasyāṃ saṃbhavati" (Mahīdhara's Hindi has "कृष्णपक्षके ६।७।८ के बीच", so the tithi range differs between the two). It quotes **Nārada**: *puratah pṛṣṭhato 'rkādyā dinarkṣaṃ lattitaṃ ca yat / arkākṛti-guṇāṅga-ṛtu(ṛṣi?)-bāṇāṣṭa-nava-saṃkhya-bham* (12, 22, 3, 6, 7, 5, 8, 9: the printed syllable is *ṛtu*, which would be 6, but the eight numbers are the eight kicks only if it is read as *ṛṣi*, 7), and **Varāha** (work not named): *sūryo dvādaśam ṛkṣaṃ ṣaṣṭhaṃ guruḥ avanijas tṛtīyaṃ tu / saṃlattayati divākara-putro 'ṣṭamam agrataḥ pādaiḥ // paścād dvāviṃśati-bhaṃ paurṇima-candras tu pañcamaṃ śukraḥ / svarbhānur api navamaṃ saptamam ṛkṣaṃ śaśāṅka-sutaḥ*, and its results (*sa eva*): *ravi-lattā vitta-harī nityaṃ kauji vinirdiśen maraṇam / cāndrī nāśaṃ kuryād baudhī nāśaṃ vadaty eva // saurī maraṇaṃ kathayati bandhu-vināśaṃ bṛhaspateḥ lattā / maraṇaṃ lattā rāhoḥ kārya-vināśaṃ bhṛgor vadati*. (Sun: loss of wealth; Mars, Saturn, Rāhu: death; Moon and Mercury: ruin; Jupiter: loss of kin; Venus: loss of work.)

**(c) Mārtaṇḍa, Vivāha, v. 16** (page 48 of the `_text.pdf`; **P**):

> भौमात् त्र्याकृतिषड्जिनाष्टनखभं हंत्यग्रतो लत्तया खेटोऽर्कार्कमितं शशी मुनिमितं पूर्णो न सन्मालवे ॥१६॥
> *bhaumāt try-ākṛti-ṣaḍ-jinā-ṣṭa-nakha-bhaṃ hanty agrato lattayā kheṭo 'rkārka-mitaṃ śaśī muni-mitaṃ pūrṇo na san-mālave.*

Counted **forward**: Mars 3, Mercury 22 (*ākṛti*), Jupiter 6, Venus 24 (*jina*), Saturn 8, Rāhu 20 (*nakha*), Sun 12, the Moon 7 (*muni*) when full. The Mārtaṇḍa's counts are **the same kicks as the Phaladīpikā and MC counted the other way**: counting round the 27 (X is the graha's own nakṣatra): back 7 (X−6) is forward 22 (X+21), back 5 (X−4) is forward 24 (X+23), back 9 (X−8) is forward 20 (X+19), back 22 (X−21) is forward 7 (X+6). So the app's two tables (`LATTA` in the Gochara code and `LATTA_KICK` in `mahadosha.ts`: Mercury −6, Venus −4, Rāhu −8, i.e. +21, +23, +19) **agree with each other and with all three texts**; `docs/sarvatobhadra-periods.md` and `muhurta-martanda-kalaprakasika.md` call this a difference, it is only notation. The Moon's kick is missing from `LATTA_KICK`, harmlessly (the Moon cannot be in the nakṣatra it kicks itself).

**(d) Nārada Saṃhitā 27.93–94** (Vivāhādhyāya; file lines 9831–9846; C): *puratah pṛṣṭhato 'rkādyā dinarkṣaṃ lattayanti ca ॥९३॥ ज्ञराहुपूर्णेन्दुसिताः स्वपृष्ठे भं सप्तगो जातिशरैर्मिते हि । संल्लत्तयन्तेऽर्कशनीज्यभौमा सूर्याष्टतर्काग्निमितं पुरस्तात् ॥९४॥* — the MC verse word for word, so Rāma Daivajña took it from Nārada. The Hindi (Abhaya Kātyāyana) adds: the Moon's kick falls "from the nakṣatra the Moon is in on the full-moon day of the month, the 22nd behind". **27.95**: *सौराष्ट्रशाल्वदेशेषु लत्तितं भं विवर्जयेत्* — the kicked nakṣatra is avoided in Saurāṣṭra and Śālva only.

**(e) The chakra text, Vyāsa v. 261–265** (lines 2479–2504; the verse text read in Khemraj and 1903 editions too, C):

> द्वादशं च तृतीयं च षष्ठं चाष्टमकं क्रमात् । लत्तयन्ति पुरोधिष्ण्यं रविभौमार्यसूर्यजाः ॥२६१॥ सप्तमे पञ्चमे धिष्ण्ये नवमे पृष्ठतः क्रमात् । बुधशुक्रस्तमो लत्तां द्वाविंशे पूर्णचन्द्रमाः ॥२६२॥ रणे मृत्युस्तथा भङ्गं यात्रायामनिवर्तनम् । विवाहे विधवा नारी भानि कुर्वन्ति लत्तया ॥२६३॥ सूर्ये तु वित्तहानिः स्यात्कुजराहुशनैश्चरैः । मरणं जीवलत्ताया बन्धुनाशो भवेत्ततः ॥२६४॥ शुक्रेण कार्यविभ्रंशो ह्यनर्थः शशिसूनुना । चन्द्रेण च महात्रासो ज्ञेयः केतुस्तु राहुवत् ॥२६५॥

Sun 12, Mars 3, Jupiter 6, Saturn 8 ahead; Mercury 7, Venus 5, Tamas (Rāhu) 9 behind, the **full** Moon 22nd. Vyāsa's Hindi on 262: "राहु तथा केतु" 9th, and "इसमें अभिजित् की गणना नहीं करनी चाहिये". Results (263): in battle death or defeat, a journey no return, a bride widowed. (264–265): Sun: loss of wealth; Mars, Rāhu, Saturn: death (*kuja-rāhu-śanaiścaraiḥ*); Jupiter: loss of kin; Venus: loss of work; Mercury: loss (*anartha*); Moon: great terror (*mahātrāsa*); **Ketu like Rāhu**. 266–270: with the lattā of the Sun on the janma nakṣatra, Mars in it: loss of place, wealth and a belly ailment, with the Moon too "great fear"; Mars's lattā with the Sun there: house broken, wealth lost, weapons; Saturn's, Rāhu's, Ketu's: disease, great fear, thieves; Mercury's: loss of wits; Jupiter's: disease; Venus's: loss of wife, urinary trouble. 271–274: the Sun's lattā on the janma nakṣatra, with the Moon there: bile, journeys, wounds; Mars: thieves; Mercury: parting from kin; Jupiter: fear of the king; Venus: parting from wife, loss of clothes; Saturn: gold, gems, cattle lost; Rāhu: *sarvasya nāśakaḥ*; Ketu: fear of weapons. 275: *उपग्रहाश्च लत्ताश्च क्रूरखेटेन संयुताः । ऋजुगत्या व्याधिकरा वक्रगत्या मृतिप्रदाः* (a malefic on the nakṣatra that has an upagraha or a lattā: disease if direct, death if retrograde). 292: a graha on the janma nakṣatra that is struck in five ways (by a malefic's vedha, aspect, an upagraha, a lattā, malefic-conjoined) kills in a month (Garga).

## 2. The Sarvatobhadra Chakra

### 2.1 Which text gives it

The chakra is given **in full** by the *Narapatijayacaryā* (Narapati, 1175 CE, chapter Sarvatobhadracakra: Narapati file lines 4702–5480) and by Vyāsa's *Trailokyadīpaka* (the same verses, with many more, and the 28 per-nakṣatra vedha lists v. 18–47), which says "पूर्वशास्त्रानुसारेण यथोक्तं ब्रह्मयामले" (v. 11; the Brahmayāmala, Śiva's, is the source). The Phaladīpikā (26.48) only refers to it: *सर्वतोभद्रचक्रोक्तं शुभवेधाः शुभावहाः । पापवेधा दुःखतरा गोचरेतांश्च चिन्तयेत्* ("the vedhas told in the Sarvatobhadra: benefic vedhas bring good, malefic ones greater pain; consider them in transit", line 17516, C), and its Hindi editor adds a summary naming *Jātakābharaṇa*, *Horāratna*, *Svaracintāmaṇi*, *Rājavijaya*. The Muhūrtacintāmaṇi and Kālaprakāśikā do **not** give this chakra (MC has the Pañca/Saptaśalākā, which is another figure). Not read: Śiva-svarodaya, Jyotiṣasāra, Jātakābharaṇa itself (identifiers found: `JatakabharanaShriKrishnaMuseumKurukshetra`, `scxy-jatakabharana-tej-kumar-book-depot`; `wdhc_swara-shastram-swara-chintamani-with-telugu-commentary…`).

### 2.2 The layout (Vyāsa v. 1–10; Narapati v. 1–9; C)

> अथातः सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि चक्रं त्रैलोक्यदीपकम् । … ऊर्ध्वगा दश विन्यस्य तिर्यग्रेखास्तथा दश । एकाशीतिपदं चक्रं … ॥ अकारादिस्वराः कोष्ठेष्वीशादौ विदिशि क्रमात् । सृष्टिमार्गेण दातव्याः षोडशेवं चतुर्भ्रमम् ॥५॥ कृत्तिकादीनि धिष्ण्यानि पूर्वाशादि लिखेत्क्रमात् । सप्त सप्त क्रमादेतान्यष्टाविंशतिसंख्यया ॥६॥ अवकहडा दिशि प्राच्यां मटपरताश्च दक्षिणे । नयभजखाश्च वारुण्यां गसदचलास्तथोत्तरे ॥७॥ त्रयस्त्रयो वृषाद्याश्च पूर्वाशादि स्थिताः क्रमात् । राशयो द्वादशैवं तु मेषान्ताः सृष्टिमार्गतः ॥८॥ शेषेषु कोष्ठकेष्वेवं नन्दादितिथिपञ्चकम् । वाराणां सप्तकं लेख्यं भौमादित्यक्रमेण च ॥९॥ भौमादित्यौ च नन्दायां भद्रायां बुधशीतगू । जयायां च गुरुः प्रोक्तो रिक्तायां भार्गवस्तथा ॥१०॥ पूर्णायां शनिवारश्च …

(Vyāsa's Hindi: Nandā east, Bhadrā south, Jayā west, Riktā north, Pūrṇā the middle. The 28 nakṣatras are seven to a side, Abhijit counted in the western seven, between Uttara Āṣāḍhā and Śravaṇa; Narapati's commentary on v. 4 says Abhijit is the last pāda of Uttara Āṣāḍhā and the first four ghaṭīs of Śravaṇa.) ### 2.3 Vedha (Vyāsa v. 12–52; Narapati v. 13–20; C)

> यस्मिन्नृक्षे स्थितः खेटस्ततो वेधत्रयं भवेत् । ग्रहदृष्टिवशेनात्र वामसम्मुखदक्षिणे ॥१३॥ वक्रगे दक्षिणा दृष्टिर्वामा दृष्टिश्च शीघ्रगे । मध्यचारे तथा मध्या ज्ञेया भौमादिपञ्चके ॥१४॥ राहुकेतू सदा वक्रौ शीघ्रगौ चन्द्रभास्करौ । गतेरेकस्वभावत्वादेषां दृष्टित्रयं सदा ॥१५॥ वामेतराऽग्रदृष्टा च वेधास्त्रिधा प्रकीर्तिताः । ऋक्षाक्षरस्वरतिथिराशिवेधश्च पञ्चधा ॥१६॥ ग्रहः सव्यापसव्येन चक्षुषा वेधयेत्पुनः । ऋक्षाक्षरस्वरादींस्तु सम्मुखेनान्त्यभं तथा ॥१७॥

Reading: from the nakṣatra a graha stands in there are **three** vedhas, left (*vāma*), front (*sammukha*), right (*dakṣiṇa*), **according to the graha's sight**. Of Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn: **retrograde → the right sight; fast (śīghra) → the left; mean (madhya) → the front.** Rāhu and Ketu are always retrograde, the Sun and Moon always fast; "from the single nature of their motion … *dṛṣṭitrayaṃ sadā*". Vedha is of five kinds: nakṣatra, letter, vowel, tithi, sign. By the left or right sight **everything on that line** is pierced; by the front **only the nakṣatra at the end** (*sammukhena antyabham*; the Hindi: "बीच में के किसी अक्षरादि को वेध नहीं होता"). Phal-H editor says the same (line 17675: "सम्मुख वेध केवल नक्षत्र का होता है। स्वर, वर्ण, राशि आदि का सम्मुख वेध नहीं होता").

**What v. 15 means is disputed between two Hindi readings (H):** Vyāsa and Narapati's *Subodhinī* (and the Gujarati/Hindi book of M. D. Patel, `oznv_sarvato-bhadra-chakra-by-mohan-bhai-d-patel…`) read "all three sights always" for the Sun, Moon, Rāhu, Ketu; the Phaladīpikā's editor (lines 17653–17664) reads one only: "राहु और केतु की सदैव वक्रगति … इनका दक्षिण वेध ही होता है। सूर्य और चन्द्रमा सर्वदा मार्गी … केवल वाम वेध ही". The Sanskrit "gater eka-svabhāvatvāt" fits the second (a single kind of motion, a single sight); *dṛṣṭitrayam* fits the first. Narapati also has another doctrine in v. 10–11 (Sun and Mars look up, Mercury and Venus aslant, Jupiter and Moon level, Saturn and Rāhu down; a graha in debility looks up, in exaltation down, between them sideways): *ऊर्ध्वदृष्टी च भौमार्कौ तिर्यग्दृष्टी बुधभार्गवौ । समदृष्टी च जीवेन्दू शनिराहू अधोदृशौ* (O/C; not in Vyāsa).

**The per-nakṣatra lists (Vyāsa v. 18–47)** give for each nakṣatra the left, right and front targets, e.g. v. 19–20: *वह्निस्थखेचरो याम्यमकारं वृषराशिनम् । नन्दाभद्रातिथी तुलां तं विशाखां श्रुतिं हरेत्* (a graha in Kṛttikā pierces **Bharaṇī by the right sight; a, Vṛṣa, Nandā, Bhadrā, Tulā, ta, Viśākhā by the left; Śravaṇa in front**). I recomputed each nakṣatra's three lines from the app's grid (a graha standing at the edge looks inward: right = clockwise side, left = the other; front = straight across) and compared them with the lists of the Hindi for Kṛttikā, Rohiṇī, Mṛgaśiras, Ārdrā, Punarvasu, Puṣya, Āśleṣā, Maghā, Hasta, Svāti, Abhijit, Śravaṇa, Dhaniṣṭhā, Revatī: **identical squares, and the right/left sides agree with the app's `diagA/diagB` once it is said which is which** (for the east edge, left = the south-west diagonal, right = north-west; for the north edge, right = the west diagonal). The others follow by symmetry. So the **geometry of `sbcVedha` is right**; what it lacks is the choice of line (below).

**Corner rule (v. 52):** *कोणस्थधिष्ण्ययोर्मध्य अन्त्यादिपादगे ग्रहे । अस्वरादिचतुष्कस्य वेधः पूर्णातिथेः क्रमात्* — a graha in the last pāda of the first and the first pāda of the second nakṣatra of a corner pair (Bharaṇī–Kṛttikā, Āśleṣā–Maghā, Viśākhā–Anurādhā, Śravaṇa–Dhaniṣṭhā) pierces the four vowels of that corner (a u ḷ o / ā ū ḹ au / i ṛ e aṃ / ī ṝ ai aḥ) and the Pūrṇā (centre) tithis. Not in the app.

**Letters (v. 48–51):** va–ba, śa–sa, ṣa–kha, ña–ṅa(?) pierced together (OCR: *बवौ शसो षखौ चैव ज्ञेयाविति परस्परम्*); ka, pa, bha, da pierce also *gha ṅa cha*, *ṣa ṇa ṭha*, *dha pha ḍha*, *tha jha ña*; a graha in Ārdrā, Hasta, Pūrva Āṣāḍhā, Uttara Bhādrapadā pierces those triples (*घङछा रौद्रगे वेधे षणठा हस्तगे ग्रहे । धफढाः पूर्वाषाढायां थझञा भाद्र उत्तरे*); the vowel pairs a–ā, i–ī, u–ū, ṛ–ṝ, ḷ–ḹ, e–ai, o–au, aṃ–aḥ are pierced together (v. 51). The app's `sbcLetterSquares` matches a single label and none of this.

**Which points are read** (v. 80, 91–100): the five of the birth: *यस्मिन्नक्षे भवेज्जन्म यो वर्णस्तत्र यः स्वरः । या तिथिस्तत्र यो राशिर्विज्ञेयं जन्मकालतः* — the birth nakṣatra, the letter (the syllable of its pāda, table v. 81–87), its vowel, the tithi, and the sign **of the nakṣatra's pāda** (nine pādas to a sign, v. 97: the Moon's sign). The name's first letter is used if the birth time is unknown (v. 98–100); a vowel-initial name takes the next letter (v. 94); a conjunct takes the first letter (v. 93); ṅa, ña, ṇa are read ga, ja, ḍa (v. 92). **The Lagna is not read** at birth (only the Lagna of a praśna, v. 212–213: *प्रश्नकाले …विद्धं यल्लग्नं क्रूरखेचरैः*). **The weekday** is written in the chakra but no verse of Vyāsa or Narapati says to read it; the only text I found that does is the Phaladīpikā editor's summary (line 17754: "जिस दिन जातक का जन्मदिन (वार) पड़े उस दिन यदि पाप वेध हो तो जातक मानसिक रूप से परेशान होता है"), without a source (H).

### 2.4 Effects (Vyāsa v. 107–152; Narapati v. 26–33; C)

Malefic vedha: one of the five points pierced: *उद्वेग*, two *भय*, three *हानि*, four *रोग*, five *मृत्यु* (v. 107). By point: nakṣatra *भ्रम* (or death of kin, disease), letter *हानि*, vowel *व्याधि*, tithi *भय*, sign *महाविघ्न* (v. 109; v. 110–118 other readings). Benefic vedha: one *सौभाग्य*, two *लाभ*, three *विजय*, four *धनसौख्य*; tithi *द्रव्यलाभ*, nakṣatra *देहवृद्धि*, sign *सुख*, letter *निर्भयता*, vowel *सौभाग्य* (v. 122–124). By graha (v. 126–152): Sun mental pain, Mars loss of wealth, Saturn disease, Rāhu and Ketu obstacles; Moon mixed (full: victory, ornaments, cure; waning: loss, grief, journeys, bonds, v. 140–143); Venus pleasure; Mercury intelligence (but if combust, with a malefic, in an enemy sign or in fall, reversed, v. 146); Jupiter all good (reversed if with a malefic); Venus with a malefic: loss of wealth and cattle. Both sides at once by two malefics (*ubhayato-vedha*, v. 220): death. A malefic leaving the nakṣatra turns its fault away (v. 139). The text dwells on **malefic and benefic vedha, which the app has**; it also gives four things the app does not apply:

* **Strength of the piercer** (v. 161–166): *स्वक्षेत्रस्थे बलं पूर्णं पादोनं मित्रभे ग्रहे । अर्धं समग्रहे ज्ञेयं पादं शत्रुगृहे स्थिते* (benefic: own sign 20, friend's 15, neutral 10, enemy's 5; the malefic's inverse, v. 164) and *वक्रग्रहे फलं द्विघ्नं त्रिगुणं स्वोच्चसंस्थिते । स्वभावजं फलं शीघ्रे नीचस्थोऽर्धफलो ग्रहः* (v. 166, Vyāsa; Narapati v. 38): **retrograde ×2, exalted ×3, fast ×1, debilitated ×½.** (Patel's Hindi version of this verse says *निष्फलो*, "no fruit", for the debilitated; Vyāsa and the Phaladīpikā editor have half.)
* **Retrograde** (v. 57): *क्रूरा वक्रा महाक्रूराः सौम्या वक्रा महाशुभाः । स्युः सहजस्वभावस्थाः सौम्याः क्रूराश्च शीघ्रगाः*. The app: "Retrograde: not weighed."
* The 5 vedhas of **bhukta, bhogya, ākrānta** nakṣatras (*dagdha, dhūmita, jvalita*, v. 101–106).
* The days: a tithi, sign, navāṁśa or nakṣatra pierced by a malefic is avoided for every auspicious work, marriage and journey (v. 180–182 = Narapati v. 39–40; Phaladīpikā editor line 17744, and Vyāsa v. 180–182).

### 2.5 Nature of the grahas (Vyāsa v. 53–57; Narapati v. 12; C)

> शन्यर्कराहुकेत्वाराः क्रूराः शेषाः शुभा ग्रहाः । क्रूरयुक्तो बुधः क्रूरः क्षीणचन्द्रस्तथैव च ॥५४॥ दशम्यवधि कृष्णे तु पक्षे पूर्णा हि चन्द्रमाः । ततः परं क्षीणचन्द्रः क्षीणः कार्यविवर्जितः ॥५५॥ [बुधः] क्रूरयुक्तो समांशके ॥५६॥

Malefics: Saturn, Sun, Rāhu, Ketu, Mars; benefics the rest; but **Mercury with a malefic is a malefic** (v. 56: in the same navāṁśa, *samāṃśake*; v. 125: "even the strength of Mercury becomes of the malefic's nature, hence Mercury with a malefic is called malefic") and the **waning Moon is a malefic**. The Hindi gloss and Narapati's *Subodhinī* put the waning Moon from Kṛṣṇa 11 to Śukla 5 (the verse itself says only "up to the tenth of the dark half it is full"). The app (`BENEFIC_GRAHAS` in `sarvatobhadraPeriods.ts`: Moon, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus; the others malefic) **differs** for the Moon (waning) and Mercury (with a malefic in one navāṁśa).

### 2.6 Verdicts for the "Transits over natal planets" table

Not found as such. The classical verdicts are: (i) the results of a graha's vedha on the five points (above); (ii) the lattā of a graha on the janma nakṣatra, alone or with grahas there (v. 266–275, quoted in §1); (iii) combinations of the Sun, Moon, Mars, Saturn standing on the janma, karma, saṅghātika, udaya, vaināśika, mānasa nakṣatras, with times of death (v. 292–315, e.g. 298: *जन्मक्षगो वा दिवसाधिनाथः कर्मक्षगो भूमिजरात्रिनाथौ । मृत्युस्तदा शत्रुकृतान्निरोधात्संपद्यते द्वादशरात्रिमध्ये*), the grahas' aspects by nakṣatra (v. 316–320: all see the 15th; Mars the 7th, 8th, 10th; Saturn 3, 5, 19; Jupiter 10, 19; Venus and Mercury 9, 12; Rāhu 9; Sun 5; benefics forward in the bright half, back in the dark, malefics the other way), and the *pakṣa/dina/kṣaṇa* grahas (v. 153–160). None counts benefics against malefics as the app's verdict does; it stays a program-derived rule.

**Results when a malefic pierces them** (Narapati v. 67–70; Vyāsa 283–285): janma: death (*मृत्युः*); karma: distress (*क्लेश*); ādhāna: exile (*प्रवास*); vināśa: quarrel with kin; sāmudāyika: ill; saṅghātika: loss; jāti: ruin of the family; abhiṣeka: bonds; deśa: ruin of the country; a benefic's vedha gives the good; with an upagraha present: death (v. 289); with a benefic on the nakṣatra, the reverse (v. 289). Piercing of vināśa, sāmudāyika, saṅghātika all three: danger to life (v. 290; Narapati v. 33).

**The nine tārās** (MC Gocara, prakaraṇa 4, v. 12, MC-H line 3153, C): *जन्माख्यसम्पद्विपदः क्षेमप्रत्यरिसाधकाः । वधमैत्रातिमैत्रा स्युस्तारा नामसहक्फलाः* — Janma 1, Sampat 2, Vipat 3, Kṣema 4, Pratyari 5, Sādhaka 6, Vadha 7, Maitra 8, Atimaitra 9, the nine counted three times (*तारा जन्मक्षात्रिरावृत्तितः*); BPHS Tārā-daśā (Hindi Khemraj line 16018): *जन्मसंपद्विपत्क्षेमप्रत्यरीसाधको वधः । मैत्रातिमैत्रमित्येवम्*. The 10th and 19th are therefore Janma again (Karma = anujanma, Ādhāna = trijanma; Phaladīpikā 26.29 "जन्मत्रय"). The Phaladīpikā 26.28 (Phal-H line 17208) names *आधान, कर्म, विपत्, जन्म, वैनाशिक, प्रत्यरि, वध* with the Hindi 19, 10, 3, 23, 5, 7.

**Verdict on the app's list** (`SBC_SPECIAL_NAKSHATRAS`): Janma 1 ✓, Karma 10 ✓, Saṅghātika 16 ✓, Sāmudāyika 18 ✓. "Naidhana 23" is the **Vināśa/Vaināśika** of the texts (the number agrees, the name does not). **"Jāti 3" and "Abhiṣeka 15" are in no text read:** the 3rd is Vipat; Jāti, Deśa and Abhiṣeka are the king's, found by the caste, the country and the day of consecration (or, in one other book, the 25th, 26th, 27th). Missing from the app: Ādhāna 19, Mānasa 25. The "red" set (Janma, Karma, Abhiṣeka) has no basis in a text: the texts give Janma death, Karma distress, Abhiṣeka bonds (king), Ādhāna exile, Vināśa quarrels, Saṅghātika loss.
